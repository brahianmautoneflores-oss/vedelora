Privacy

This notice explains how information associated with visits, purchases and customer support may be handled.

Version 1.0 · Effective September 2026

1. Information you provide

When you purchase a license or contact VEDELORA, information such as your name, email address, billing details, order information and messages may be processed as necessary to complete the transaction, provide support, maintain records and comply with legal obligations.

2. Payments

Payments are processed through an authorized third-party commerce and payment provider. VEDELORA does not need to receive or store your complete payment-card number. The provider may process personal and payment information under its own privacy terms and legal obligations.

3. Website data

The website or its hosting and commerce providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps and basic usage data for security, reliability, fraud prevention and service operation. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced later, this notice will be updated as appropriate.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to fulfill orders, deliver licensed files, answer customer requests, prevent abuse or fraud, maintain transaction records and comply with applicable legal or accounting obligations.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process transactions, deliver purchases, provide customer support or comply with law. VEDELORA does not sell customer personal information as a standalone business activity.

6. Retention and rights

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable legal requirements. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain personal information, subject to lawful record-retention requirements.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to the contact address provided by VEDELORA on the website, checkout or purchase receipt.

This notice may be updated when VEDELORA adds new commerce, analytics or communication services. The effective date above identifies the current version.
